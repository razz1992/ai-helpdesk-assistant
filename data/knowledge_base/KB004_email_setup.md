# Corporate Email Setup and Troubleshooting

## Initial setup
1. Connect the device to the internet.
2. Open the approved corporate email application.
3. Add the corporate email account.
4. Complete corporate sign-in and MFA.
5. Allow the mailbox to synchronize.

## Troubleshooting
- **Cannot sign in:** Confirm the corporate account works in the Identity Portal. If locked, contact IT Support.
- **Email is not syncing:** Check internet connectivity, restart the email application and verify offline mode is disabled.
- **Mailbox storage warning:** Remove unnecessary items or follow approved retention guidance.
- **New device setup:** Use the approved email application and follow device enrollment requirements.

## Ticket information
Include the email application, device operating system, approximate start time and non-sensitive error text.

## Security
Never include passwords, MFA codes or confidential message contents in a support ticket.
