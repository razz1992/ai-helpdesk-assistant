# Laptop and Corporate Access Provisioning

## Request process
1. Open the IT Service Portal.
2. Choose **Hardware Request** for a laptop or **Access Request** for an application.
3. Select the required device or application.
4. Enter business justification and required-by date.
5. Submit the request for manager approval.
6. After approval, IT fulfils the request and updates the ticket.

## Approval
Manager approval is required before a laptop or restricted application access is provisioned. Some applications may require additional application-owner approval.

## Fulfilment
The service desk reviews the request, checks approvals and availability, and assigns work to the fulfilment team. The ticket is the source of truth for request status.

## Urgent access
Do not bypass approval controls. Contact the service desk and explain business impact and deadline.
